## Full About — EN

### Who we are

**Sylphx Limited** builds **Sylphx** — a cloud company whose services are bought à la carte — and contributes open infrastructure under **SylphxAI**. Sylphx Cloud is the hosting composer and marketplace front door: it hosts and provisions the services you choose, without implementing or proxying a peer's API. Identity, Data, Compute, Events, Observability, and Commerce stand alone on their own sites, APIs, credentials, and SDKs. The company's AI products live in the **Sylphx AI** family — Sylphx Models and Sylphx Agents — each an independent product under the hub `https://sylphx.ai/`.

### Why we exist

The modern starter stack is a tax — not because any one tool is bad, but because the pieces don't know about each other. Every vendor brings its own identity story, isolation model, and bill; the glue between them is nobody's job. Agent systems make the tax worse.

Sylphx exists so builders can buy the pieces they need from one company that builds them to compose — independently when that is all you need, assembled through Cloud when you want the front door.

### What we believe

1. **Independent beats bundled.** Buy the pieces you need; every product stands alone.  
2. **Composition is a product.** Cloud owns the hosting and provisioning seams; peers stay independently purchasable and callable.  
3. **Isolation should be real.** Per-project data boundaries you can reason about.  
4. **Idle should be cheap.** Hibernate when nothing runs; wake fast when it does.  
5. **AI is a family, not a feature.** Models and Agents are first-class products, not a bolt-on.  
6. **Open proof matters.** Libraries and MCP tools show how we engineer.

### Customer Zero

We run our own product and agent systems on Sylphx — through the same public contracts and admission as any customer. The platform is not theoretical.

### Who we’re for

Indie builders, startups, and AI product engineers who want to buy precisely what they need and have it compose; teams that care about isolation and cost when projects sleep; developers who live in MCP and TypeScript ecosystems.

### Who we’re not for

Buyers who want a single bundled SKU covering everything, or enterprises that need a multi-year ERP replacement story on day one. We are a cloud for builders who ship software.
