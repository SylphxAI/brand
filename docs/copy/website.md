## Problem

**H2:** Your stack isn't the problem. The gaps between the pieces are.  

**Body:** Vendor glue is where the time and money go: a different identity story, isolation model, and bill from every tool — and nobody accountable for the seams. Sylphx builds every product to the same composition rules: public contracts and credentials, honest failure reasons, and Cloud owning the assembly when you want it.
